# Pest Management for Nigerian Farmers

Integrated Pest Management (IPM) is the best approach for managing pests safely and effectively.

## Common Pests
- **Fall Armyworm:** Attacks maize. Control by planting early, weeding, and applying bio-pesticides or neem extract.
- **Stem Borers:** Affect maize, rice, and sorghum. Destroy crop residues after harvest.
- **Aphids:** Affect vegetables. Use soapy water sprays or ladybugs.
- **Whiteflies:** Transmit Cassava Mosaic Disease. Use resistant varieties to manage the disease.

## Organic vs Chemical Control
- **Organic Control:** Uses natural predators (birds, spiders), neem oil, wood ash, and garlic sprays. Safe for the farmer and the environment.
- **Chemical Control:** Use synthetic pesticides only as a last resort. Always wear protective gear (gloves, masks, long sleeves) when spraying. Do not spray near water sources.
