# Yam Production Guide for Nigeria

Yam is a high-value crop, primarily grown in the derived savanna and forest zones of Nigeria.

## Seed Preparation
- Use healthy seed yams or yam setts weighing 150g-250g.
- Treat setts with wood ash or recommended fungicide to prevent tuber rot.

## Planting and Staking
- Plant on mounds or ridges. Mounds should be about 1 meter apart.
- Plant between February and April.
- Staking is crucial, especially in forest zones, to expose leaves to sunlight. Use bamboo or strong branches, 2-3 meters high.

## Weeding and Fertilizer
- Keep the farm weed-free, especially in the first 3-4 months.
- Apply NPK fertilizer at a rate of 400kg/ha, placed in a ring around the mound.

## Harvesting and Storage
- Harvest when the leaves turn yellow and dry up.
- Store in traditional yam barns with good ventilation. Tie tubers to live poles to prevent contact with the ground and reduce rotting.
