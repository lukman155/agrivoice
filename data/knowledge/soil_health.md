# Soil Health for Nigerian Farmers

Healthy soil is the foundation of successful farming. Without good soil, fertilizers and seeds will not perform well.

## Soil Testing
- Always test your soil before planting to know its pH and nutrient levels. 
- You can contact your local ADP extension officer for affordable soil testing.

## Improving Soil Health
- **Composting:** Use crop residues, animal manure, and kitchen waste to make compost. It improves soil structure and water retention.
- **Crop Rotation:** Do not plant the same crop on the same piece of land year after year. Rotate cereals (like maize) with legumes (like cowpea or soybeans) to naturally fix nitrogen in the soil.
- **Cover Cropping:** Plant melon (egusi) or sweet potato to cover the soil and prevent erosion.
