# Cassava Farming Guide for Nigeria

Cassava is a staple crop in Nigeria. It is highly drought-tolerant and performs well in marginal soils.

## Planting
- Use healthy stem cuttings (about 20-25 cm long) from disease-free mature plants (8-18 months old).
- Plant cuttings at an angle of 45 degrees or vertically.
- Spacing should be 1m x 1m.
- The best time to plant is at the onset of the rainy season (March/April in the South, May/June in the Middle Belt).

## Weed and Fertilizer Management
- Weed the farm 3-4 weeks after planting, and again at 8 and 12 weeks.
- Apply NPK 15:15:15 fertilizer at the rate of 400kg/hectare about 8 weeks after planting.

## Pests and Diseases
- **Cassava Mosaic Disease (CMD):** Caused by a virus and spread by whiteflies. Use resistant varieties like TME 419.
- **Cassava Bacterial Blight (CBB):** Use disease-free stems.

## Harvesting
- Harvest 9-18 months after planting, depending on the variety.
- Cut stems and gently uproot to avoid bruising tubers.
