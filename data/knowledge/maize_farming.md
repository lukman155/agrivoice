# Maize Farming Guide for Nigeria

Maize (corn) is widely cultivated across all agro-ecological zones in Nigeria. 

## Planting
- Select high-yielding and disease-resistant seeds (e.g., Oba Super 6).
- Plant when rains are fully established.
- Plant 2-3 seeds per hole at a depth of 2-5 cm. Space at 75cm x 25cm.

## Fertilizer
- Apply NPK 15:15:15 at planting or 2 weeks after planting.
- Top-dress with Urea at 4-6 weeks after planting.

## Pests
- **Fall Armyworm (FAW):** A devastating pest. Use approved bio-pesticides or chemical insecticides. Neem extracts are a good organic alternative.
- **Stem Borers:** Apply systemic insecticides if infestation is severe.

## Harvesting
- Harvest for dry grains when the silk turns brown and moisture content drops to about 18-20%.
- Store in airtight bags (like Purdue Improved Crop Storage - PICS bags) to prevent weevil attacks.
