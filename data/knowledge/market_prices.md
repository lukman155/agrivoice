# Market Information and Selling Guide for Farmers

Maximizing profit requires good market knowledge and proper timing.

## Best Practices for Selling
- **Do not rush to sell immediately after harvest** when prices are lowest. If possible, store grains safely using PICS bags and wait a few months for prices to increase.
- **Value Addition:** Processing your produce (like grating and frying cassava into garri) increases your profit margin significantly compared to selling raw tubers.

## Cooperative Marketing
- Join a local farmers' cooperative. Cooperatives can pool resources to transport goods directly to major urban markets (like Mile 12 in Lagos or Dawanau in Kano), avoiding middlemen who take most of the profit.
- Cooperatives also give you better bargaining power for bulk sales.
